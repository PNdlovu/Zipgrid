## Vision

> **"To become the operating system for EV charging at home and at work — starting in the UK, scaling to Europe and beyond."**

Zipgrid envisions a world where every homeowner with a charger and every business with a parking bay is part of a living, intelligent charging network. Where your charger knows when electricity is cheapest, books its own repairs, and earns you money while you sleep — without you lifting a finger.

---

## Mission

> **"To leapfrog dated charging apps by combining peer-to-peer charging, voice control, and agentic AI into one platform — so homeowners and small businesses in the UK can finally take full control of their EV charging infrastructure."**

---

## The Leapfrog Thesis

The UK EV charging app market is growing but functionally stagnant. The incumbents — Zap-Map, Pod Point, BP Pulse, Shell Recharge — were built for a simpler era. They monitor. They map. They don't think, act, or connect.

Zipgrid leapfrogs them not by doing the same things better, but by doing things they cannot do at all:

| What incumbents do | What Zipgrid does instead |
|--------------------|--------------------------|
| Show you charger status | Act on it — auto-schedule, auto-report, auto-fix |
| Accept tap inputs | Respond to natural voice commands |
| List public chargers | Enable private homes and businesses to become chargers |
| Sell charger hardware separately | Integrate hardware commerce into the platform |
| Provide a single app | Provide an EV energy OS — the hub for everything charging |

This is not incremental. It is a category shift.

## Strategic Goals

### Phase 1 — Foundation (0–6 months)
- Launch in London with 500 active listings (homeowners + SMBs)
- Reach 5,000 registered users (drivers + hosts combined)
- Complete 2,000 charging sessions/month
- Voice + agentic automation live for homeowner tier
- OCPP integration supporting top 5 UK charger brands (EO, Rolec, Andersen, Ohme, Zappi)
- Stripe Connect payout infrastructure live
- Platform insurance policy placed with underwriter
- Platform NPS > 50

### Phase 2 — Growth (6–18 months)
- Expand to Manchester, Birmingham, Bristol, Edinburgh
- 2,500 active listings, 25,000 registered users
- Launch SMB Portal with multi-charger dashboard
- Launch Marketplace (installer bookings + hardware)
- Launch Fleet / Corporate accounts
- Octopus Energy smart tariff integration live
- Revenue: £2M ARR
- Breakeven on unit economics

### Phase 3 — Scale (18–36 months)
- National UK coverage — top 20 cities
- 10,000 active listings, 100,000 registered users
- OCPI protocol live (roaming interoperability)
- Grid demand data product sold to utilities
- Series A raise: £5M–£10M
- Revenue: £6M–£10M ARR
- Begin EU expansion (Ireland, Netherlands, Germany)

### Phase 4 — Platform (36+ months)
- Zipgrid positioned as the "EV Energy OS" — not just an app
- White-label licensing to property developers and councils
- Carbon credit marketplace layer
- V2G (Vehicle-to-Grid) session support
- EU presence: 5+ markets
- Exit optionality: acquisition interest from utility, OEM, or energy tech acquirer

## Strategic Moats

| Moat | Why it's defensible |
|------|---------------------|
| **Voice + agentic AI layer** | Cannot be cloned quickly by legacy infrastructure companies. Requires deep ML investment and UX rethinking. |
| **Network density flywheel** | More hosts → more bookings → more host earnings → more hosts. Classic marketplace compounding. |
| **OCPP device data** | Session telemetry from paired devices creates a proprietary dataset no mapping app can replicate. |
| **Installer & vendor network** | Once certified installers build workflows around Zipgrid, switching cost is high. |
| **Trust infrastructure** | Insurance, KYC, and verified reviews take years to build credibility. Competitors cannot shortcut this. |
| **Energy supplier integrations** | Bundled deals with Octopus/EDF create a distribution moat — Zipgrid pre-installed for EV tariff customers. |
| **Host earnings lock-in** | A homeowner earning £200/month from their driveway does not churn. |
