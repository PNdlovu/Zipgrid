## Vision

> **"To become the AI-powered marketplace for EV charging, parking, and mobility — starting in the UK, scaling to Europe and beyond."**

Zipgrid envisions a world where every homeowner with a charger and every business with a parking bay is part of a living, intelligent network. Where your charger knows when electricity is cheapest, books its own repairs, and earns you money while you sleep — and where every driver can find a trusted space whether they need to charge, park, or both — without ever lifting a finger.

## Mission

> **"To leapfrog dated charging apps by combining peer-to-peer charging, voice control, agentic AI, and a parking marketplace into one platform — so homeowners, small businesses, and EV drivers in the UK can take full control of their mobility and energy needs."**

---

## The Leapfrog Thesis

The UK EV charging app market is growing but functionally stagnant. The incumbents — Zap-Map, Pod Point, BP Pulse, Shell Recharge — were built for a simpler era. They monitor. They map. They don't think, act, or connect.

Zipgrid leapfrogs them not by doing the same things better, but by doing things they cannot do at all:

| What incumbents do | What Zipgrid does instead |
|--------------------|--------------------------|
| Show you charger status | Act on it — auto-schedule, auto-report, auto-fix |
| Accept tap inputs | Respond to natural voice commands |
| List public chargers | Enable private homes and businesses to become chargers |
| Sell charger hardware separately | Integrate hardware commerce into the platform |
| Provide a single app | Provide an EV mobility OS — charging, parking, trip planning, and energy in one place |

This is not incremental. It is a category shift.

## The Energy Access Marketplace — Long-Term Vision

*Added in Planning Baseline v2.2 — September 30, 2026*

### Why "EV Charging" Is the Wrong Frame for the Long Game

Most competitors define themselves by the hardware:
> "We operate chargers."
> "We map chargers."
> "We install chargers."

Zipgrid defines itself by the outcome:
> **"We remove effort from energy access."**

Today that means EV charging. But the infrastructure Zipgrid is building — a trusted host network, an AI agent layer, a wallet, a marketplace, a community — is indifferent to the specific energy product being exchanged.

The natural progression:

```
Phase 1–2  →  EV Charging Marketplace
              P2P sessions · voice · agentic AI · host earnings

Phase 3    →  EV Mobility OS
              Charging + parking + trip planning + OCPI public network

Phase 4    →  Energy Access Marketplace
              EV charging + home battery surplus + solar export sharing
              + V2G grid services + smart charging hubs + energy sharing
              between neighbours
```

When a homeowner has a Zappi, a solar panel array, and a home battery — they are sitting on an energy asset. Today they have no intelligent way to monetise or optimise it. Zipgrid becomes the AI layer that does exactly that, automatically.

**This is not a pivot.** It is the same product extended to adjacent energy flows that use the same infrastructure, the same wallet, the same agent, and the same trust network already built for charging.

---

### The Concierge Reframe

Every product decision at Zipgrid should be filtered through one question:

> **"Does this remove effort from the user's energy life?"**

Not: "Does this add a feature?"  
Not: "Does this improve the map?"  
Not: "Does this show more data?"

The concierge framing means:

| Old way (search-first) | Zipgrid way (outcome-first) |
|------------------------|----------------------------|
| "Find me a charger near Manchester" | "I'm going to Manchester tomorrow — handle the charging" |
| "Check if this charger is available" | Agent checks, books, and confirms before you ask |
| "Set a schedule for off-peak charging" | Agent schedules automatically based on tariff and battery SoC |
| "How much have I spent this month?" | Agent surfaces the insight unprompted when it detects unusual spend |
| "My charger is broken" | Agent detects the fault from OCPP telemetry, diagnoses it, and contacts the installer — before the host even knows |

**The screen becomes optional. The agent is the product.**

### Vertical Markets — Where the Concierge Framing Wins Fastest

The brainstorm with the founding team identified two verticals where Zipgrid's founder has personal domain expertise that no competitor can replicate quickly:

#### Care Homes

A typical care home site has:
- Staff car park with EVs (nurses, carers, management)
- Family visitor parking (EVs increasingly common)
- Management vehicles
- Potential community/public access after hours

Current reality: chargers installed but idle 70%+ of the time, generating zero revenue, with no system to manage access, pricing, or staff vs. visitor separation.

Zipgrid for Care Homes:
- Multi-bay listing management (SMB Portal)
- Access segmentation: staff bay / visitor bay / public bay — each with separate pricing and access rules
- AI auto-pricing based on demand patterns (visiting hours = premium; night = off-peak public access)
- ESG reporting for CQC compliance and sustainability reporting
- Fleet accounts for care company vehicle reimbursement
- Emergency charging for on-call staff

**Founder advantage:** Direct operational knowledge of care home workflows, CQC requirements, staffing patterns, and procurement processes. No competitor has this.

#### Warehouses & Logistics Sites

A typical warehouse or logistics site has:
- Employee car park (shift workers, office staff)
- Visitor/client parking
- Operational fleet (LGVs, vans, smaller EVs)
- Underused overnight capacity
- Sustainability reporting requirements (Scope 3 emissions, fleet decarbonisation)

Current reality: EV infrastructure installed for compliance or sustainability optics but managed manually, revenue opportunity ignored entirely.

Zipgrid for Warehouses:
- Shift-aware scheduling — charge employee vehicles during shift, release to fleet/public between shifts
- Fleet account integration — multi-vehicle, spend policy, monthly invoice
- One charger, multiple revenue streams: employee use (subsidised), visitor use (PAYG), fleet use (fleet account), public access (nights/weekends)
- AI manages all of it autonomously — no manual configuration per shift
- ESG data product — Scope 3 fleet emissions reporting, carbon saved vs petrol fleet
- Demand response participation — warehouse sites often have grid connection capacity to earn from demand response

**Founder advantage:** Operational knowledge of warehouse workflows, shift patterns, logistics fleet management, and Scope 3 reporting requirements.
