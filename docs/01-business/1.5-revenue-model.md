## 1. Revenue Architecture

Zipgrid operates a multi-stream revenue model. No single stream dominates in Year 1 — the mix shifts toward higher-margin subscriptions and marketplace commissions as the network scales.

```
Revenue Streams
│
├── 1. P2P Transaction Fees          ← Primary (Year 1–3)
├── 2. Homeowner Subscriptions       ← Primary (Year 1–3)
├── 3. SMB Subscriptions             ← Primary (Year 1–3)
├── 4. Marketplace Commissions       ← Growth (Year 2–3)
├── 5. Idle / Overstay Fees          ← Supplementary (Year 1+)
├── 6. Enterprise / Fleet Contracts  ← Scale (Year 3+)
└── 7. Grid Data Licensing           ← Future (Year 3+)
```

## 8. Exit Scenarios

Zipgrid is positioned for strategic acquisition as the network scales:

| Acquirer Type | Rationale | Estimated Multiple |
|--------------|-----------|-------------------|
| Energy supplier (Octopus, EDF, EOn) | Distribution channel, customer data, P2P network | 8–12x ARR |
| Charger OEM (EO Charging, Wallbox) | Software layer for hardware sales; subscription revenue | 6–10x ARR |
| Property / mobility platform (Rightmove, Uber) | EV charging as feature layer on existing platform | 5–8x ARR |
| Public charging network (bp pulse, ChargePoint) | Homeowner / SMB market they cannot reach organically | 8–15x ARR |
| IPO (long-term) | If scale reaches £30M+ ARR and network density is national | Market-rate |

At £6.5M ARR in Year 3 with a 10x multiple, exit value range: **£50M–£100M**.

## New Revenue Streams

### Stream 8 — Wallet Float Interest
The Zipgrid Wallet holds driver pre-loaded credit (Stripe Customer Balance). At scale, the aggregate float generates interest income.

| Metric | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Active Wallet users | 8,000 | 30,000 |
| Average wallet balance | £12 | £18 |
| Total float (£) | £96,000 | £540,000 |
| Assumed interest rate (Bank of England base ±1%) | 4.5% | 4.5% |
| **Annual float interest revenue** | **£4,320** | **£24,300** |

> Float interest is modest early but meaningful at scale. Primary value of the Wallet is retention and friction reduction, not interest income. FCA/HMRC wallet classification must be confirmed by legal counsel — if classified as e-money, FCA authorisation would be required. Stripe Customer Balance model avoids this in most configurations (pre-payment for own services, not stored for general use).

---

### Stream 9 — Driver Premium Subscription (Zipgrid Plus)
A monthly subscription tier for frequent drivers offering perks that justify the fee. Distinct from host Smart subscription.

| Tier | Price | What's Included | Target Audience |
|------|-------|----------------|----------------|
| **Free** | £0 | Basic search, booking, session monitoring | Occasional drivers |
| **Zipgrid Plus** | £4.99/month | Priority booking (instant book always enabled even on non-instant listings) · 1.5× rewards points · 5% Wallet top-up bonus · Trip planner unlimited stops · No booking convenience fee | Frequent drivers (≥ 4 sessions/month) |
| **Zipgrid Plus Annual** | £49.99/year | Same as Plus, ~17% discount | Committed drivers |

**Unit economics — Zipgrid Plus:**

| Metric | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Plus subscribers | 3,000 | 12,000 |
| Average price (blended monthly + annual) | £4.20/month | £4.20/month |
| **Monthly subscription revenue** | **£12,600** | **£50,400** |
| **Annual subscription revenue** | **£151,200** | **£604,800** |

**Conversion assumption:** 12% of active drivers (≥ 2 bookings in last 30 days) convert to Plus.

### Stream 10 — Parking Marketplace Commission
When the Parking Marketplace launches in Phase 3, Zipgrid takes a commission on parking-only and parking+charging bundle transactions.

| Transaction Type | Commission Rate | Notes |
|-----------------|----------------|-------|
| Parking-only booking | 12% of transaction value | Same rate as P2P charging |
| Parking + charging bundle | 12% of parking portion + existing charging commission | Split by transaction component |
| Airport parking (daily rate) | 10% of daily rate | Lower rate for longer-duration bookings |
| Event parking | 15% of transaction value | Higher rate — event premium pricing |
| Monthly parking permit | 8% of monthly fee | Recurring; lower rate for volume |

**Parking Marketplace revenue projections (Phase 3+):**
