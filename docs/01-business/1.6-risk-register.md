## 1. Risk Scoring Matrix

| Likelihood | Definition |
|-----------|-----------|
| **High (3)** | Likely to occur within 12 months without mitigation |
| **Medium (2)** | May occur within 12–24 months |
| **Low (1)** | Unlikely but possible |

| Impact | Definition |
|--------|-----------|
| **Critical (4)** | Business failure, significant legal liability, or safety incident |
| **High (3)** | Major revenue loss, regulatory action, or reputational damage |
| **Medium (2)** | Operational disruption, moderate financial loss |
| **Low (1)** | Minor inconvenience, quickly recoverable |

**Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact**

| Score | Rating | Action |
|-------|--------|--------|
| 9–12 | 🔴 Critical | Immediate action required — board-level escalation |
| 6–8 | 🟠 High | Active mitigation plan in place — reviewed monthly |
| 3–5 | 🟡 Medium | Monitored — reviewed quarterly |
| 1–2 | 🟢 Low | Accepted — reviewed annually |
