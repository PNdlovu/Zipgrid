## Why Verticals Matter

Generic B2C growth (one homeowner at a time) has a high CAC and a slow ramp. Vertical B2B partnerships collapse the supply acquisition problem — one deal with a 50-site care home group delivers 50 listings with a single MSA signature.

More importantly, vertical expertise creates a moat that pure-tech competitors cannot shortcut. The founding team's operational knowledge of care homes and warehouse/logistics environments means Zipgrid speaks the language of the buyer, understands their compliance requirements, and knows their workflow pain points in a way that a team of software engineers never will.

**Rule:** Every vertical playbook must answer three questions:
1. What does this site have today (the problem)?
2. What does Zipgrid give them tomorrow (the solution)?
3. What number do they care about most (the hook)?

## Vertical 1 — Care Homes

### 1.1 The Opportunity

The UK has approximately 15,700 registered care homes (CQC, 2024). A typical medium-sized care home (50 beds) has:
- 10–40 parking bays
- 1–4 existing EV chargers (often installed for OZEV grant compliance)
- Staff car park (nurses, carers, kitchen, admin, management)
- Visitor parking (family members, 80%+ weekend peaks)
- Management/company vehicles
- No system to manage access, pricing, or bay separation between groups

**Current state:** Chargers are installed, underused, generating zero revenue, and managed manually (if at all). Staff sometimes argue over access. Visitors don't know there's a charger available. Management vehicles compete with staff for priority bays.

**Zipgrid state:** AI manages everything. The care home manager does nothing.

---

### 1.2 The 3-Number Hook

When approaching a care home operator, lead with three numbers:

```
"Your charger sits idle for roughly 14 hours a day.
 At the platform average of 2.8 sessions per charger per day,
 you could earn £150–£400 per charger per month — passively."
```

Then: "We set it up. The AI runs it. You collect the earnings."

---

### 1.3 Access Segmentation — The Core Feature

Care homes require strict access separation that generic platforms cannot support. Zipgrid's listing model supports this natively via the access_type field and time-based availability rules.

**Recommended configuration:**

| Bay Type | Access | Pricing | Hours | Managed By |
|----------|--------|---------|-------|------------|
| Staff bays (2–4) | Fleet account — named staff only | Subsidised or salary sacrifice | 06:00–22:00 | Fleet account auto-billing |
| Visitor bays (2–4) | PAYG — QR code at bay | Market rate (28–36p/kWh) | 09:00–20:00 | Zipgrid auto |
| Management bays (1–2) | Reserved — specific vehicle plates | Employer-subsidised | 07:00–19:00 | Fleet account |
| Public access | Open PAYG via app | Off-peak rate (20–26p/kWh) | 20:00–08:00 | Zipgrid auto |

No staff member manages any of this. The AI handles access codes, pricing transitions, conflict resolution, and payouts automatically.

---

### 1.4 ESG & Compliance Value

Care homes face increasing ESG reporting pressure from:
- **CQC (Care Quality Commission):** Environmental sustainability now part of the Well-Led domain assessment
- **NHS procurement requirements:** Suppliers to NHS need evidence of carbon reduction plans
- **Local authority contracts:** Many council-funded placements now require ESG statements
- **Family choice:** Families choosing care homes increasingly consider environmental reputation

Zipgrid automatically generates:
- Monthly session reports: kWh delivered, sessions, CO2 saved
- Annual ESG summary: comparable to SECR format
- Per-session carbon data (g CO2e saved vs petrol equivalent)

This turns a passive charger infrastructure into an active ESG evidence document — at no extra work for the care home.

---

### 1.5 Emergency Charging for On-Call Staff

A feature no competitor has and care homes need:

On-call nurses and carers sometimes arrive with low batteries outside normal hours. Zipgrid's emergency charging mode (`emergency_sessions` table) allows:
- On-call staff to activate emergency access via app at any time
- Access granted automatically to the nearest available bay
- Session logged against their staff fleet account
- Platform fee waived (feature: `platform_fee_waived = true` for emergency sessions)

This is a genuine safety feature that care home operators can present to staff unions and CQC inspectors as evidence of staff welfare investment.

---

### 1.6 Sales Approach

**Champion identification:** Target sustainability lead, facilities manager, or HR director — not IT, not finance first.

**Conversation opener:**
> "You've already made the investment in EV infrastructure. We help you earn from it and report on it — without adding any work to your team."

**Proof point:** One pilot site, 4-week trial, real earnings deposited to the care home's bank account via Stripe Connect payout.

**Contract structure:**
- Master Services Agreement: signed by HQ, covers all sites in group
- Per-site activation: group pays one SMB subscription per site activated
- Revenue share: standard 10–15% platform commission on all sessions
- ESG export: included in SMB Pro tier (£100/month)

**Objection handling:**

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Our staff need priority access" | "Staff bays are ring-fenced — no driver can book them. AI enforces it." |
| "We don't want strangers on site" | "Visitor and public bays are separate. Access codes expire per session. CCTV integration available." |
| "We don't have time to manage this" | "You don't manage it. The AI does. Your involvement is zero after setup." |
| "What about GDPR?" | "All data stored in EU (Railway Amsterdam). Full GDPR DPA available. No personal data shared with third parties." |
| "We already have a charger management app" | "We integrate with your existing charger via OCPP. You don't lose your existing setup." |

## Vertical 2 — Warehouses & Logistics

### 2.1 The Opportunity

The UK has approximately 1,500 large logistics warehouses (100,000+ sq ft) and tens of thousands of smaller industrial and distribution sites. The shift to EV fleets is accelerating due to:
- UK HGV zero-emission mandate (2040, with van ZEV mandate 2030)
- Company car tax incentives pushing employees toward EVs
- SECR (Streamlined Energy & Carbon Reporting) requiring Scope 3 fleet data
- Logistics contracts with Tier 1 retailers (Tesco, ASDA, M&S) now requiring Scope 3 evidence

Most sites have installed EV chargers — but the chargers are managed with the same rigour as office water coolers: they exist, nobody is responsible for them, and they generate no value.

---

### 2.2 The 3-Number Hook

```
"Your chargers run at about 30% utilisation.
 The other 70% of the time — nights, weekends, shift gaps —
 they could earn you £800–£2,400 per month per charger.
 The AI manages all of it. Your team does nothing."
```

For the sustainability manager:
```
"We give you a monthly Scope 3 Scope 3 Category 7 report
 automatically. No consultants. No manual data collection.
 Just the report, every month."
```

---

### 2.3 Shift-Aware Scheduling — The Killer Feature

Warehouses run 24/7 on rotating shifts. The AI Commute Agent learns shift patterns (entered once at setup) and automatically:

**Example: 3-shift warehouse (Days/Lates/Nights)**

```
06:00–14:00   Day shift
  → Staff bays reserved 05:30–06:15 (early arrival buffer)
  → Bays released at 14:15 for afternoon visitor/fleet use

14:00–22:00   Late shift
  → Staff bays reserved 13:45–14:15
  → AI charges fleet vehicles overnight: 22:00–05:00

22:00–06:00   Night shift
  → Staff bays reserved 21:45–22:15
  → Public PAYG access: 06:15–13:45 (highest public utilisation window)

Bank holidays / weekends
  → All bays switch to public PAYG mode
  → AI applies weekend premium pricing (+20%)
```

This logic is entered once during onboarding. The AI maintains it indefinitely, adjusting automatically when shift patterns change (host can update via voice or SMB portal).

---

### 2.4 Multiple Revenue Streams from One Charger

The pitch to a warehouse facilities manager:

```
One charger. Four revenue streams. Zero management.

1. Employee fleet account  →  salary sacrifice / expense reimbursement
                              automated, HMRC-compliant rate
                              invoiced monthly per employee

2. Visitor PAYG            →  QR code at bay entrance
                              no staff involvement, auto-invoiced

3. Public access window    →  nights and weekends
                              AI sets price, takes payment, deposits earnings

4. Demand response         →  earn grid incentives in Phase 3
                              by adjusting charge rate during peak events
                              National Grid ESO programme
```

### 2.5 SECR & Scope 3 Reporting Automation

**What SECR requires (annually):**
- Total energy consumption (kWh) from all business activities including transport
- Scope 1 (direct combustion), Scope 2 (purchased electricity), Scope 3 (indirect — employee commuting, business travel)

**What Zipgrid delivers automatically:**
- Per-session: driver ID, kWh, timestamp, CO2e (grid intensity × kWh), vehicle
- Monthly rollup: total kWh by category (employee/fleet/visitor/public)
- Annual SECR summary: formatted for direct inclusion in annual report
- GHG Protocol Category 6 (business travel) and Category 7 (employee commuting) line items

**Value statement:**
> "Your sustainability consultant charges £150/hour to extract this data from RFID logs and spreadsheets. Zipgrid gives it to you automatically, every month, for free as part of your subscription."

---

### 2.6 Fleet Reimbursement Automation

For warehouse operators with company car or grey fleet policies:

- Each employee links their personal EV to their fleet account
- Sessions charged at home or at work log automatically
- At month end, HMRC-compliant reimbursement report generated per employee
- Payroll integration (future Phase 3): direct deposit to payroll system

This removes the biggest friction point in corporate EV adoption — the complexity of home charging reimbursement.

---

### 2.7 Sales Approach

**Champion identification:** Start with the sustainability manager or fleet manager. Finance director becomes the economic buyer once the revenue / cost-saving case is presented.

**Pilot structure:**
- 1 site, minimum 4 chargers, 90 days
- Zipgrid activates site in 1 day (remote OCPP pairing)
- Month 1 report delivered automatically — first tangible proof point
- Month 3 review: present earnings, utilisation improvement, SECR data to site manager and sustainability lead
- Roll-out proposal to all company sites

**Key differentiator vs. fleet-only SaaS (Mina, Fuuse, WattsUp):**
> "They manage fleet charging costs. We manage fleet charging costs AND generate public/visitor revenue AND produce your SECR report AND connect to the national demand response programme. For the same price."

## Vertical 3 — Hotels & Hospitality

### 3.1 The Opportunity

UK hotels are installing EV chargers as a guest amenity — but treating them as a cost centre. A typical 80-room hotel with 4 chargers might have:
- 30–40% guest EV penetration (growing rapidly)
- Chargers available 24/7 but managed PAYG with no booking system
- No revenue from public access during low-occupancy periods
- No dynamic pricing — fixed rate regardless of demand

**Zipgrid state:**
- Guest-only booking window (linked from hotel booking confirmation email)
- Public PAYG access during 02:00–10:00 low-occupancy periods
- Premium pricing Friday/Saturday evenings (leisure demand peaks)
- Monthly revenue deposited automatically to hotel bank account

---

### 3.2 The Hook

```
"Four chargers, 35% occupancy. At optimal pricing and scheduling,
 those chargers earn you £400–£900/month — passively.
 And every EV driver guest sees that you have guaranteed reserved
 charging before they book their room."
```

The second point matters: guaranteed charging access is increasingly a booking decision factor. Hotels that offer reserved EV charging (not just "a charger exists") differentiate meaningfully in OTA listings.

---

### 3.3 OTA Integration (Phase 3)

Future integration: Zipgrid listing appears as an amenity detail on Booking.com and Expedia:
- "4 EV charging bays — bookable in advance via Zipgrid"
- Driver books hotel room → booking confirmation includes Zipgrid charging link
- Seamless: one flow, two bookings

This creates a distribution advantage no competitor can offer.

---

### 3.4 Target Hotel Segments

| Segment | Why | Phase |
|---------|-----|-------|
| Independent hotels (50–150 rooms) | Fast decision-making, sustainability-motivated | 1–2 |
| Boutique chains (Bespoke Hotels, Exclusive Hotels) | Brand-conscious, amenity-focused | 2 |
| Roadside lodges (Premier Inn, Travelodge) | Scale — one deal, hundreds of sites | 3 (corporate procurement) |
| Glamping / rural stays | Guest EV anxiety is highest on rural trips | 2 |

## Vertical 4 — Churches, Sports Clubs & Community Venues

### 4.1 The Opportunity

The long-tail supply opportunity. The UK has:
- ~40,000 churches (many with car parks idle 5–6 days per week)
- ~40,000 sports clubs (car parks empty on weekday mornings)
- ~10,000 community halls and village halls
- ~5,000 golf clubs and leisure centres

These venues have one thing in common: **large car parks that sit empty for 60–80% of the time**, and increasingly, chargers installed via OZEV community grant schemes.

Individual venue revenue is modest (£50–£200/month). At scale (10,000 venues × £100/month average), this is £1M+/month of platform-mediated GMV from a supply segment no competitor is pursuing.

---

### 4.2 The Hook

```
"Your car park earns nothing on Tuesday mornings.
 List your charger on Zipgrid.
 Local EV drivers book it while you're not using it.
 You earn. You do nothing."
```

For a church treasurer:
```
"It's a new gift aid-eligible income stream for the building fund."
```

For a sports club secretary:
```
"The members who drive EVs get to charge during training.
 Non-members can book when the club is quiet.
 The AI handles it."
```

---

### 4.3 Distribution Strategy

Community venues are best reached through their representative bodies, not individual outreach:

| Body | Membership | Approach |
|------|------------|----------|
| National Churches Trust | 5,500 historic churches | Partnership → newsletter feature |
| Sport England | 1,000s of registered clubs | Community charging programme |
| Action with Communities in Rural England | Village halls | Rural EV charging initiative |
| Golf club associations (England Golf) | 1,800+ golf clubs | Sustainability programme |
| UK Active | 4,000+ leisure centres | EV charging benefit for members |

One partnership with Sport England could put Zipgrid in front of every registered sports club in England in a single email. This is the long-tail supply strategy.

---

### 4.4 Product Configuration

Community venues use the simplest Zipgrid tier:
- **Homeowner Smart (£7.99/month)** for single charger
- **SMB Starter (£25/month)** for venues with 2–5 chargers
- No fleet accounts required
- No shift scheduling required
- AI handles everything: availability windows, pricing, access codes, payouts

Setup time: 30 minutes. No installer required if charger is already OCPP-paired.
