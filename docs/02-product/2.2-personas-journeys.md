## Persona 1 — The Homeowner Host

### Profile
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Name** | Sarah, 42 |
| **Location** | Earlsfield, South London |
| **Occupation** | Secondary school teacher |
| **Vehicle** | Nissan Leaf 40kWh (2021) |
| **Charger** | Ohme Home Pro (7.4kW Level 2, installed 2022) |
| **Tech comfort** | Medium — uses iPhone, Alexa at home, comfortable with apps |
| **EV tariff** | Octopus Go (off-peak 7p/kWh, 12am–6am) |

### Goals
- Recoup the £800 she paid for charger installation
- Charge her own car cheaply overnight — ideally automatically
- Not deal with strangers blocking her driveway or causing issues

### Frustrations Today
- The Ohme app is confusing — she set a schedule once and isn't sure it's still running
- She knows her charger sits idle Mon–Fri 8am–4pm while she's at school but has no way to earn from it
- When the charger threw an error last month she had to call Ohme support and wait 3 days for a callback
- She has to manually remember to plug in and check the app each night

### What Zipgrid Does for Sarah
- **One app for everything** — monitoring, scheduling, and P2P earnings regardless of charger brand
- **"Charge before 7am"** voice command while she's making breakfast — done
- **Earns £140/month** on average from drivers booking her driveway Mon–Fri
- **Fault → fixed automatically** — when her charger showed an error, Zipgrid detected it, diagnosed a loose connector, and booked a local NICEIC-certified installer for the next day

## Persona 2 — The Small Business Owner

### Profile
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Name** | Dev, 51 |
| **Location** | Didsbury, Manchester |
| **Business** | Café + bakery, 22 covers, 8-space car park |
| **Chargers** | 2× Rolec WallPod EV (7.2kW each), installed 2023 |
| **Tech comfort** | Low-medium — uses Android, WhatsApp, Square POS; avoids complexity |
| **Motivation** | His regular customers are increasingly arriving in EVs. He wants to offer charging as a reason to stay longer and spend more. |

### Goals
- Know exactly how much money the chargers are generating
- Not deal with the administration of managing access and payments himself
- Stop customers from just parking at the chargers without charging

### Frustrations Today
- The Rolec app shows him basic session data but no revenue view
- He has no way to prevent ICE vehicles from parking at the charger bays (short of a sign)
- He tried to set a price but the Rolec admin portal was too complex
- He doesn't know which customers are EV drivers vs. parking abusers

### What Zipgrid Does for Dev
- **Single dashboard** showing revenue per charger, utilisation rate, and dwell time
- **AI pricing suggestion** based on time-of-day demand: "Enable surge pricing 12–2pm (lunch rush) to earn 22% more"
- **QR code** on each charger — customers scan to start; non-EV drivers can't "use" the bay without the app
- **Idle fee enforcement** — after session ends, fees deter lingering
- **Monthly report** delivered by email — no need to open the app

### Profile
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Name** | Andy, 38 |
| **Location** | South Birmingham |
| **Business** | Solo OZEV-certified EV charger installer, trading for 3 years |
| **Volume** | ~6 installs/month; wants to grow to 12 |
| **Tech comfort** | Medium — uses WhatsApp, Checkatrade, basic accounting software |
| **Pain point** | Checkatrade leads are expensive (£80–£120/month subscription) and low quality. Word-of-mouth is his best source but unpredictable. |

### Goals
- Consistent, local, high-quality job leads
- A platform that verifies his credentials so customers trust him without long sales conversations
- Simple job management — quote, confirm, invoice, done

### What Zipgrid Does for Andy
- **Marketplace profile** with OZEV badge prominently displayed
- **Agentic referrals** — when a Zipgrid platform fault is detected at a charger near Birmingham, Andy gets a notification: "Fault detected at host charger 2.1 miles away — interested in the job?"
- **Fixed-price service packages** (basic install, repair, survey) — no quoting friction for simpler jobs
- **Escrow payments** — paid via platform when job marked complete; no chasing invoices
- **12% commission** vs. £100/month Checkatrade with no performance guarantee

## Persona 5 — The Fleet Manager (Phase 2)

### Profile
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Name** | Claire, 46 |
| **Location** | Bristol |
| **Company** | Regional property maintenance firm, 18 vans (12 now EVs) |
| **Situation** | Company switched half the fleet to EVs but has no central way to manage charging costs or ensure drivers are charging efficiently |
| **Pain point** | Drivers are using public rapid chargers at £0.79/kWh when there are cheaper alternatives. Monthly charging bill is unpredictable and hard to reconcile. |

### Goals
- Consolidated monthly invoice for all EV charging
- Policy controls: preferred charger types, spending limits, approved locations
- Know which drivers are charging inefficiently

### What Zipgrid Does for Claire
- **Fleet account** with all 12 EVs registered
- **Spend analytics** by driver, vehicle, and location
- **Policy controls**: restrict to Level 2 only (no rapid) within company budget
- **Monthly invoice** (single payment; Zipgrid handles everything)
- **Integration with accounting** (Xero/QuickBooks export — Phase 2)
