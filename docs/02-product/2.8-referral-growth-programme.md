## 1. Philosophy

Word of mouth is the highest-trust, lowest-cost acquisition channel. A homeowner who earns £140/month from Zipgrid telling their neighbour is worth more than any paid ad.

The referral programme rewards the behaviour that drives organic growth — and makes the business economics work because every referred user has a significantly higher activation rate and lower CAC than paid acquisition.

**Rule:** The incentive must be meaningful enough to motivate action but cost-controlled enough not to erode unit economics. A free month is visible, valuable, and time-bounded — it costs Zipgrid £7.99 (homeowner) or £25–100 (SMB) in deferred revenue, but acquires a user who may stay for years.

## 2. Programme 1 — Friend Referral (Homeowners & Drivers)

### How It Works

**Referrer (existing user):**
- Gets a unique referral link from their profile (Share → Copy link / WhatsApp / Email)
- When their referred friend completes their **first qualifying action**, the referrer earns **1 free month** of their current subscription

**Referred friend (new user):**
- Clicks the referral link → lands on a co-branded page: *"[Name] invited you to Zipgrid"*
- Signs up and completes the qualifying action
- Gets **£5 Wallet credit** credited immediately on signup (not conditional — immediate goodwill)

### Qualifying Actions by Role

| Referred User Role | Qualifying Action | Referrer Reward |
|-------------------|------------------|-----------------|
| Homeowner | Publishes their first active listing | 1 free month of Smart tier |
| Driver | Completes their first charging session | 1 free month of Driver Plus |
| Installer | Completes their first marketplace job | 1 free month of marketplace listing fee |

### Conditions

```
1. Referred user must be a genuinely new user (no existing account)
2. Referral must be made through the official referral link (not a coupon code)
3. Qualifying action must be completed within 60 days of signup
4. Referrer must be on a paid tier at the time of reward (free users earn wallet credit instead)
5. Maximum 10 successful referrals per calendar month per account (anti-abuse cap)
6. Referral reward is non-transferable and non-cashable (Wallet credit or subscription credit only)
7. Both parties must remain active accounts — if referred user refunds/cancels within 30 days, reward is reversed
8. Referral credit applied to next billing cycle automatically
```

### Anti-Abuse Rules

```
BLOCKED:
  - Same IP creating referrer + referee accounts
  - Same device fingerprint for both accounts
  - Same payment method used for both accounts
  - Referee email domain matches referrer's (corporate email abuse)
  - More than 3 referrals from the same IP in 7 days
  - Account < 30 days old cannot refer (prevents disposable account abuse)

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW:
  - Referrer earning > 5 free months in 30 days
  - Referee completes qualifying action within 24 hours of signup (suspicious speed)
  - Multiple referrals to accounts with similar names or email patterns
```

### Referral Tracking (Technical)

```typescript
// Referral link format:
// https://zipgrid.co.uk/join?ref=SARAH-XK72

// referral_code: 8-char unique code per user
// Stored in: users.referral_code (generated at registration)
// Tracked in: referral_events table (referrer_id, referee_id, status, qualifying_action_at, reward_issued_at)

type ReferralStatus =
  | 'pending'          // referee signed up, qualifying action not yet done
  | 'qualified'        // qualifying action completed
  | 'reward_issued'    // free month credited
  | 'reversed'         // referee cancelled/refunded within 30 days
  | 'expired'          // 60-day window passed without qualifying action
  | 'flagged'          // anti-abuse review
```

## 3. Programme 2 — Business Bring-a-Business

SMBs often own multiple premises, belong to business associations, or know other business owners. This programme rewards commercial hosts who bring other businesses onto the platform.

### How It Works

**Referring Business Host:**
- Brings another business (café, hotel, gym, etc.) to Zipgrid
- The referred business registers with a business account and activates at least 2 chargers on the platform

**Reward Structure:**

| Referred Businesses Brought | Reward to Referring Business |
|-----------------------------|------------------------------|
| 1 business activated | 1 free month of current SMB subscription |
| 2 businesses activated | 2 free months |
| 3 businesses activated | 3 free months |
| 4 businesses activated | 4 free months |
| 5 businesses activated | 6 free months (2-month bonus) |
| 10+ businesses activated | 12 free months (full year free) |

**Cap:** Maximum 12 months free per referring business per year. After that, a negotiated enterprise partnership arrangement (see below).

### Enterprise Partnership (10+ referrals)

For businesses or business associations that bring 10+ SMBs:
- 12 months free subscription (all tiers)
- Dedicated account manager
- Co-marketing opportunity (joint PR, "powered by Zipgrid" branding)
- Revenue share discussion if they are acting as a formal reseller

### Conditions for Business Bring-a-Business

```
1. Referred business must be a new Zipgrid business account (no prior account)
2. Referred business must have a valid Companies House / business registration number
3. Referred business must activate a minimum of 2 chargers on the platform
4. Activation means: at least 1 completed real booking on that charger within 60 days
5. Maximum 1 referral reward per referred business (cannot be referred by multiple businesses)
6. Referring business must be on a paid SMB tier at time of activation
7. Free months are applied sequentially — not simultaneously
8. Free months expire if the referring business cancels their account
9. Fraud or misrepresentation voids all accrued rewards
```

### Anti-Abuse Rules (Business Programme)

```
BLOCKED:
  - Referring business and referred business share the same director/owner
    (detected via Companies House director check)
  - Same registered address
  - Same VAT number or company group
  - Referred business exists but has not genuinely adopted the platform
    (< 5 sessions in 90 days = reward reversed)

FLAGGED:
  - Bulk business referrals submitted within 7 days (possible coordinated abuse)
  - Referred businesses in the same postcode cluster (possible fake business ring)
```

## 4. Programme 3 — Driver Refer-a-Driver (Guerrilla)

Simple, low-friction, share after every session.

**Trigger:** Immediately after a session summary screen, if driver's satisfaction is inferred as positive (completed session, no complaint, charged > 10 kWh):

```
"Love Zipgrid? Share it and both of you get £5 Wallet credit."
[Share with a friend →]  [Maybe later]
```

No subscription required. Both parties get £5 Wallet credit when the referred driver completes their first session. No tier requirements. Maximum 20 referrals per driver per year via this mechanism.

## 6. Financial Modelling

### Cost per Referral

| Programme | Cost (deferred revenue) | Acquired User LTV | ROI |
|-----------|------------------------|------------------|-----|
| Homeowner refers homeowner | £7.99 (1 free month Smart) | £320 (3-year LTV) | 40× |
| Driver refers driver | £5 Wallet credit | £144 (2-year LTV) | 28× |
| SMB refers SMB (5 brought) | £300 (6 months free at £50/mo) | £1,800 (3-year LTV) | 6× |

**Conclusion:** Even the most generous tier (SMB bring-a-business) returns 6× LTV vs. referral cost. This is significantly better than paid acquisition CAC of £25–£30 per SMB host.

## 7. Launch Mechanics

**Pre-launch:**
- Waitlist referral: *"Move up the waitlist — get 5 friends to join and unlock early access + 3 months free"*
- No subscription needed — just email capture

**At launch:**
- Referral programme live from Day 1
- All existing waitlist members get their referral link on launch day
- Push notification to all registered users: *"Your referral link is ready — share it and earn"*

**Referral leaderboard (opt-in):**
- Top 10 referrers per month shown on a public leaderboard (first name + city only)
- Top referrer each month gets a feature in the monthly email and social media shoutout
