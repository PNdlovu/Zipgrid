## 1. Support Tiers

### Tier 1 — Self-Service
**What:** Help centre articles, in-app AI Q&A, onboarding tooltips
**Who handles:** Automated (no human)
**Target resolution:** Immediate
**Volume:** ~60% of issues resolved here

### Tier 2 — Standard Support
**What:** General enquiries, billing questions, booking issues, account settings
**Who handles:** Support agent (chat + email)
**SLA:** First response within 4 hours (business hours) · Resolution within 24 hours
**Hours:** Mon–Fri 8am–8pm, Sat 9am–5pm (UK time)

### Tier 3 — Specialist / Escalation
**What:** Disputes, incidents, KYC failures, complex payout issues, insurance claims
**Who handles:** Senior support agent or ops team
**SLA:** First response within 2 hours · Resolution within 3–5 business days
**Hours:** Mon–Fri 9am–6pm

### Tier 4 — Critical / Safety
**What:** Physical safety incidents, active session emergencies, data breaches
**Who handles:** On-call ops team + leadership
**SLA:** Response within 15 minutes (24/7)
**Trigger:** "Emergency" flag in-app or email subject line `URGENT:`

## 3. Ticket Categories & Scripts

### 3.1 Session Didn't Start

**Likely causes:** Charger offline, OCPP pairing issue, wrong arrival code, booking window not open yet

**Script:**
> "Thanks for getting in touch. Let's get this sorted quickly. A few things to check:
> 1. Are you within your booked time window? Sessions can only start from the scheduled start time.
> 2. Try scanning the QR code on the charger again — sometimes a second scan triggers the connection.
> 3. Check your arrival code in the app under 'My Bookings' → tap your booking → 'View Access Details'.
> If none of those work, tap 'Report a Problem' on your booking and our team will contact the host immediately. If you're waiting, your session time will be adjusted automatically."

**Escalation trigger:** Charger offline in OCPP dashboard → ops team contacts host within 30 minutes.

---

### 3.2 Session Stuck / Not Stopping

**Likely causes:** OCPP service issue, cable not disconnected, app/connectivity issue on driver side

**Script:**
> "Your session is showing as active on our system. Here's what to do:
> 1. Try tapping 'Stop Charging' in the app again.
> 2. If the charger has a physical stop button, press it once.
> 3. Physically unplug the cable from your vehicle — this will send an automatic stop signal.
> The energy consumed up to the point you stopped will be what you're charged. If there's any discrepancy, reply here and we'll review the session logs."

**If session cannot be stopped remotely:** Ops team triggers manual stop via OCPP service admin tool.

---

### 3.3 Overcharged / Billing Dispute

**Script:**
> "I can see your session on [date] at [listing name]. Here's the full breakdown:
> - Energy delivered: [X] kWh
> - Rate: £[X]/kWh
> - Session cost: £[X]
> - Idle fee: £[X] ([Y] minutes × £0.10/min)
> - Total: £[X]
>
> [If correct]: This matches your session data exactly. If you believe there's still an error, please let me know which part looks wrong and I'll investigate the raw meter logs.
>
> [If error found]: You're right — there's a discrepancy. I'm issuing a refund of £[X] now. You'll see it on your card within 5–10 business days."

**Refund authority:** Support agents can issue refunds up to £50 without manager approval. Above £50: escalate to Tier 3.

---

### 3.4 Payout Not Received (Host)

**Script:**
> "Payouts are processed every Monday for the previous week's completed sessions (Monday–Sunday). Here's what I can see for your account:
> - Your next payout of £[X] is scheduled for [date]
> - Bank account on file: [last 4 digits] [sort code ending]
>
> If the payment date has passed, it can take 1–3 business days to appear depending on your bank. If it's been more than 5 business days since the scheduled date, I'll raise this with our payments team right now."

**Escalation trigger:** Missing payout > 5 business days → Tier 3 → payments team → Stripe investigation.

---

### 3.5 KYC Verification Failed

**Script:**
> "Our identity verification partner wasn't able to confirm your details this time. The most common reasons are:
> - Document was blurry or partially obscured — try a well-lit, flat surface
> - Document was expired
> - Selfie didn't match the document photo — ensure you're in good lighting and remove glasses
>
> You can try again in the app under Profile → Identity Verification. You have [X] attempts remaining.
>
> If you continue to have difficulty, reply here with your query (do not send document images by email) and I'll arrange a manual review."

---

### 3.6 Host Reporting Damage by Driver

**Script:**
> "I'm sorry to hear this happened. Here's what we'll do:
> 1. I'm logging a formal incident report on your behalf right now (Incident #[ID]).
> 2. Please upload photos of the damage through the 'Report a Problem' button in your booking — this creates a secure, timestamped evidence record.
> 3. If the damage exceeds £500, this may be covered by the Zipgrid Host Protection Guarantee. Our team will assess and guide you through the process within [X] hours.
> 4. If you need to involve the police for significant damage or theft, please do — we'll need the crime reference number for the insurance process."

---

### 3.7 Driver Reports Unsafe Charger

**Script:**
> "Your safety is our priority. Please do not use the charger if you have any safety concerns.
>
> I'm immediately placing the listing under review — no other drivers will be able to book it until we've investigated.
>
> Could you describe what you observed? [Burns, sparks, unusual smell, physical damage, cable issue?]
>
> If you're concerned about immediate physical danger, please move away from the area and call 999 if necessary.
>
> Our ops team will contact the host within the next [30 minutes] to arrange a safety inspection."

**Action:** Immediately pause listing in admin panel. Alert ops team. Create incident report.

---

### 3.8 Account Suspended (User Appealing)

**Script:**
> "Your account was suspended on [date] due to [brief reason, e.g. 'a pattern of overstay fees' or 'a billing dispute that was found to be fraudulent'].
>
> If you believe this decision was made in error, you can submit a formal appeal within 14 days by replying to this message with:
> 1. Why you believe the suspension was incorrect
> 2. Any supporting information
>
> Our Trust & Safety team will review your appeal within 5 business days and respond with a final decision."
