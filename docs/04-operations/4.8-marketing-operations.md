## 1. Marketing Philosophy

Zipgrid's marketing has one job: **get homeowners to list their charger and get EV drivers to book it.** Everything else is secondary.

The platform has a supply problem first (hosts), then a demand problem (drivers). Marketing sequencing reflects this:

```
Month 1–2:  Host acquisition only (supply seeding)
Month 3:    Driver acquisition begins (supply > 300 listings)
Month 4+:   Dual-sided growth (host + driver simultaneously)
Month 8+:   SMB and marketplace marketing begins
```

**Marketing principle:** Every pound spent must have a traceable path to a host activation or a completed booking. Vanity metrics (impressions, followers) are tracked but never the headline KPI.

## 3. SEO Strategy

SEO is the highest-ROI long-term channel. Every listing page is SSR'd and indexable.

### 3.1 Keyword Strategy

**Host acquisition (supply) — high-intent transactional:**
```
"earn money from home EV charger UK"       — 2,400 searches/month
"rent out EV charger UK"                   — 1,800 searches/month
"how to make money from EV charger"        — 1,200 searches/month
"OZEV charger home income"                 — 800 searches/month
```

**Driver acquisition (demand) — local intent:**
```
"EV charger near me London"               — 8,100 searches/month
"cheap EV charging London"               — 4,400 searches/month
"book EV charger in advance"             — 1,600 searches/month
"private EV charger hire UK"             — 900 searches/month
```

**Installer acquisition:**
```
"OZEV certified installer London"         — 1,200 searches/month
"EV charger installer near me"           — 3,600 searches/month
```

**Informational (top-of-funnel — builds trust and email list):**
```
"how much does EV charging cost UK"      — 12,000 searches/month
"Octopus Agile EV charging guide"        — 4,400 searches/month
"best home EV charger UK 2027"          — 6,600 searches/month
"EV range in cold weather UK"           — 2,400 searches/month
```

### 3.2 Programmatic SEO — Listing Pages

Every charger listing is a unique indexable page:

```
/chargers/london/earlsfield/level-2-ccs-11kw
/chargers/london/hackney/level-2-type2-7kw
/chargers/manchester/didsbury/level-2-covered
```

At 500 listings, Zipgrid has 500 unique indexed pages with genuine content (photos, reviews, availability, price). At 10,000 listings, this creates a search moat that no competitor can replicate without the listings themselves.

City landing pages:
```
/chargers/london          — "EV Chargers in London"
/chargers/manchester      — "EV Chargers in Manchester"
/chargers/birmingham      — "EV Chargers in Birmingham"
```

### 3.3 Content Marketing

**Blog topics (Month 1–6):**
```
"How Sarah earns £140/month from her Earlsfield driveway"  (host story)
"The real cost of public EV charging in London"            (driver pain)
"Octopus Agile explained: when to charge cheaply"          (SEO magnet)
"Best Level 2 home EV chargers UK 2027"                    (comparison)
"How restaurants are earning from EV charging"             (SMB angle)
"EV range in winter: what nobody tells you"               (problem-aware)
```

**Tools (interactive — drive organic links + email capture):**
```
"EV Charging Cost Calculator"      — input kWh + rate → show savings vs. public
"Host Earnings Estimator"          — input availability + location → estimated monthly earnings
"Charging Time Calculator"         — input battery % + charger kW → time to full
"Range Impact Calculator"          — input temperature → adjusted range estimate
```

### 3.4 Technical SEO Requirements

- SSR listing pages (React Server Components — not client-rendered)
- `sitemap.xml` auto-generated from active listings database
- `robots.txt` permits indexing of listing + city pages
- `hreflang` tags for future multi-locale
- Open Graph + Twitter Card meta for social sharing
- Schema.org `LocalBusiness` markup on listing pages
- Core Web Vitals: LCP < 2.5s, CLS < 0.1, FID < 100ms (enforced in CI)

## 4. Paid Acquisition

Paid turns on at Month 3 — once we have 300+ listings and a proven conversion funnel.

### 4.1 Google Ads

**Campaign 1 — Driver intent (high-intent, bottom-of-funnel):**
- Keywords: "book EV charger London", "reserve EV charging near me"
- Landing page: map search pre-filtered to user's location
- Target CPA: < £8 per registered driver
- Budget: £3,000/month (Month 3), scaling to £15,000/month (Month 12)

**Campaign 2 — Host intent:**
- Keywords: "earn from home EV charger", "rent out charger UK"
- Landing page: host landing page with earnings calculator
- Target CPA: < £25 per activated host listing
- Budget: £2,000/month (Month 3)

**Campaign 3 — Installer intent:**
- Keywords: "EV installer marketplace UK", "get EV installation jobs"
- Landing page: installer signup page
- Budget: £500/month (Month 9 — marketplace launch)

### 4.2 Meta (Facebook/Instagram)

**Audience:** UK EV owners (target: recent new car purchase + EV ownership signals)

**Creative formats:**
- Video: 15-second "earn from your driveway" hook (homeowner story)
- Carousel: "3 chargers near you in Earlsfield from £0.32/kWh"
- Story: voice command demo — tap to see "Hey Zipgrid, find me a charger"

**Budget:** £2,000/month (Month 3), growing to £10,000/month (Month 12)

### 4.3 Retargeting

- All visitors who viewed a listing but didn't book → retarget with that listing + review highlight
- All hosts who started listing creation but didn't publish → retarget with earnings estimate for their area
- Budget: 15% of total paid budget

## 6. Email Marketing

### 6.1 Automated Sequences

**Host onboarding sequence (8 emails over 14 days):**
```
Day 0:  "Welcome — your listing is live" (confirmation)
Day 1:  "Add more photos — listings with 5+ photos get 3× more bookings"
Day 3:  "Check your AI pricing suggestion"
Day 5:  No booking yet? "Here's what top-earning hosts in [city] do differently"
Day 7:  "First booking received! You earned £X" (conditional send)
Day 7:  No booking yet → "Let's troubleshoot your listing together" (conditional)
Day 10: "Set up peak pricing for evenings and weekends"
Day 14: "Your first week: here's how you compare to other [city] hosts"
```

**Driver activation sequence (5 emails over 7 days):**
```
Day 0:  "Welcome — here's a charger near you"
Day 1:  "3 chargers within 0.5 miles of [location]" (personalised with PostHog location)
Day 3:  "Your first booking guide" (how-to, short)
Day 5:  "Voice tip: try saying 'Book my nearest CCS charger for tomorrow'"
Day 7:  No booking → "£5 Wallet credit — your first booking on us"
```

**Weekly digest (ongoing — opt-in):**
- Host: weekly earnings summary + next payout date
- Driver: "Chargers near [home area] this week — new listings"

### 6.2 Transactional Emails (All must be polished, not plain-text)

```
booking_confirmed.html      — QR code, details, access instructions, map
session_completed.html      — kWh, cost, range added, receipt download link
payout_sent.html            — earnings breakdown, next payout date
review_received.html        — star rating preview, host response prompt
incident_opened.html        — case reference, next steps, evidence upload link
wallet_topup.html           — confirmation, new balance, recent transactions
rewards_milestone.html      — tier upgrade, new perks, points balance
```

## 7. Referral Programme

### 7.1 Driver Referral
- Share a personal link ("Book your first session with £5 off")
- Referrer earns 500 Rewards points when referred friend completes first booking
- Both parties benefit — referred driver gets £5 Wallet credit

### 7.2 Host Referral
- Host refers another homeowner
- Both earn £10 Wallet credit when referred host completes first active booking
- "Tell your neighbour" campaign — literal neighbourhood density flywheel

### 7.3 Installer Referral Channel
- Installer recommends Zipgrid at point of installation
- Host activates within 30 days → installer earns £20 referral credit
- Tracked via unique installer referral codes
