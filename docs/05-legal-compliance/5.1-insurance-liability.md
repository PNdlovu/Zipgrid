## 0. Summary of the model

**Zipgrid does not insure hosts or drivers.** Zipgrid is the booking and payment platform. Hosts keep their own home/buildings and public liability cover that permits charger sharing; drivers keep the motor insurance the law requires. Zipgrid protects both sides through verification, secured payment, a complete session record, a dispute process with fixed deadlines, and **damage recovery from drivers** (up to £1,000 per case, paid to the host in full).

This is what the product says and does today:

| Where | What it says |
|-------|--------------|
| `/legal/host-terms` | Host Terms and Protection — cover hosts need, what Zipgrid does, damage recovery, exclusions |
| `/legal/driver-terms` | Driver Responsibilities and Liability — rules of use, what drivers are/aren't liable for, how claims work |
| `/legal/terms`, `/legal/privacy` | Terms of Service and Privacy Policy |
| `/host/settings/insurance` | Insurer notification letter; "Zipgrid does not provide insurance" |
| `/safety` | Public explanation of the model |

**Why not a platform "Host Protection Guarantee" (v0.1)?**
- Arranging or providing insurance for hosts is an FCA-regulated activity unless structured carefully (Airbnb's AirCover is partly a guarantee, partly a policy held by Airbnb). It needs a broker and legal opinion before it can be offered or advertised.
- A $1M–$5M umbrella with $0 host excess is a material cost before there is any loss history to price it.
- Damage recovery from the driver at fault, plus the host's own insurer for large losses, gives hosts a real remedy now without either problem.

A guarantee can be added later (see §6) without changing the product's claim flow, because claims already go through one Resolution Centre case with a captured evidence record.

## 1. Risk Landscape

| Risk | Likelihood | Severity | Who bears it | Mechanism |
|------|-----------|----------|--------------|-----------|
| Driver damages charger, cable, holster | Medium | Low–Medium | Driver | Damage recovery (≤ £1,000); above that, host's insurer → subrogation |
| Driver damages property (wall, gate, landscaping) | Low | Medium | Driver | As above |
| Vehicle collision on host property | Low | Medium | Driver | Driver's motor policy |
| Electrical fault damages a vehicle | Low | High | Host | Host's public liability; manufacturer if a product defect |
| Slip/trip over cable, injury | Medium | Medium | Host (premises) | Host's public liability |
| Charger fire / electrical hazard | Very low | Critical | Host / installer / manufacturer | Host's buildings + public liability |
| Billing dispute / overcharge | Medium | Low | Platform | Resolution Centre refund |
| Driver doesn't pay (shortfall, damage charge) | Low | Low | Platform (residual bad debt) | Wallet → card collection; booking block |
| Data breach | Low | High | Platform | Zipgrid's own cyber cover (§5) |
| Platform error causing loss (wrong charge, wrong access code) | Low | Medium | Platform | Zipgrid's professional indemnity / CGL (§5) |

## 2. Responsibilities

| Party | Responsible for |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Host** | Safe, certified installation (BS 7671, RCD, qualified electrician), maintenance, accurate listing, own insurance and telling their insurer, reporting damage within 14 days |
| **Driver** | Correct, careful use; compatible cables only; no tampering; staying within booking; reporting problems within 24 hours; paying for damage they cause |
| **Zipgrid** | ID verification of both sides, payment secured before arrival, arrival codes and session PINs, session record, acknowledging cases (1h safety / 24h other), impartial review, damage recovery and payment to host, audit trail, data protection |

## 3. Claim flow (implemented)

```
Incident ─▶ 999 first if anyone is in danger
   │
   ▼
Resolution Centre case (driver OR host, from the booking)          DisputeService.open
   │  • raised against the other party, who is notified
   │  • booking + vehicle + charging-session snapshot captured   (disputes.session_snapshot)
   │  • acknowledge_by = +1h (safety_incident) / +24h (others)
   │  • property_damage only within 14 days of booking end
   ▼
Both sides add evidence (photos, video, quote/invoice)            dispute_evidence
   ▼
Admin queue — safety first, then overdue acknowledgements          /admin/disputes
   ▼
Decision
   ├─ refund driver (wallet or card)
   ├─ uphold host damage claim → charge driver ≤ £1,000           DisputeService.openDamageCharge
   │     collected wallet → saved card (payment_shortfalls kind='damage')
   │     credited to host 100% as it is collected                  earnings_allocations reason 'damage:*'
   │     unpaid charge blocks new bookings
   ├─ escalate (injury, > £1,000, fire) → host's / driver's insurers get the evidence package
   └─ account action (warning, suspension)
   ▼
Appeal within 14 days via support (case number)
```

**Evidence package for insurers** (what we can hand over on request): case description and both parties' statements, uploaded evidence, session snapshot (times, energy, peak power, stop reason, fault codes, charge point and connector), booking (times, arrival code use), verified identities, policy versions each party accepted (`policy_acceptances`).

**Not covered by damage recovery:** pre-existing damage, wear and tear, faults in the host's own installation, lost earnings, vehicle damage, events outside anyone's control (power cuts, storms, floods).

## 4. Host Onboarding Safety Requirements (implemented)

Before a listing goes live, hosts:
- [x] Declare installation by a qualified electrician (NICEIC / NAPIT / ECA) and RCD protection, and the install year (safety checklist → Safety Score)
- [x] Accept the Host Terms and Protection (recorded per listing and in `policy_acceptances`)
- [x] Verify their identity (Stripe Identity)
- [ ] Upload the Electrical Installation Certificate (planned; currently self-declared)
- [ ] Charger photo showing make/model and cable condition (planned as a listing requirement)

Listings are scored daily; below 50 they are paused for review.

## 5. Zipgrid's own insurance (to arrange — broker)

These protect **Zipgrid the company**, not hosts or drivers:

| Cover | Why | Indicative limit |
|-------|-----|------------------|
| Public / products liability (CGL) | Claims that the platform itself caused injury or damage (e.g. wrong access instructions) | £5M |
| Professional indemnity / tech E&O | Platform errors: wrong charges, failed payouts, bad guidance | £1M–£2M |
| Cyber liability | Data breach response, notification, regulatory defence | £1M–£2M |
| Employers' liability | Legal requirement once staff are employed | £10M (statutory min £5M) |
| Directors & officers | Investor expectation | £1M |

Broker shortlist: marketplace / sharing-economy specialists (e.g. Marsh, Aon, Superscript, Hiscox for PI/cyber). Underwriters will ask for: transaction volume projections, average session length and energy, host safety requirements (§4), the claim flow (§3), incident history, and the published terms.

## 6. Optional later: Host Protection Guarantee

If hosts need more than damage recovery, the next step is a guarantee funded by Zipgrid (or a policy held by Zipgrid for hosts' benefit) that pays the host when the driver cannot be recovered from, up to a fixed limit. Before offering it:
1. FCA perimeter advice — guarantee vs insurance distribution.
2. Broker quote for the backing policy, priced on actual claim history from damage recovery.
3. Update `/legal/host-terms` (bump `policies.ts` version so hosts re-accept).

## 7. Data Liability

| Data Type | Risk | Mitigation |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| Payment card data | PCI DSS breach | Stripe handles — no raw card data on Zipgrid servers |
| Identity documents | Regulatory breach | Stripe Identity; Zipgrid stores only the result |
| Location data | Privacy breach | UK GDPR retention and deletion (`/legal/privacy`) |
| Access codes | Unauthorised property access | Per-booking codes and session PINs, valid only for the booking |
| Session logs and case evidence | Misuse | Admin-only access, audit log; cases kept 6 years |

## 8. UK regulatory notes (launch market)

| Area | Regulation | Impact |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Installation | BS 7671 (IET Wiring Regulations) — Section 722 for EV charging | Host requirement; Electrical Installation Certificate |
| Charge points | Electric Vehicles (Smart Charge Points) Regulations 2021 | Applies to devices sold in GB since 2022; hosts should use compliant units |
| Consumers | Consumer Rights Act 2015; Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 | Reasonable care and skill; cancellation rights |
| Data | UK GDPR, Data Protection Act 2018, PECR (cookies) | Privacy policy, ICO registration, essential-only cookies |
| Insurance | FSMA 2000 / FCA perimeter | Do not offer or describe cover for hosts without advice (§0, §6) |
| Payments | Stripe as regulated payment provider | Zipgrid does not hold client money outside Stripe and the wallet ledger |

Other markets (EU AFIR, US NEC 625, South Africa NRS 097 / POPIA, Brazil LGPD) need a local review before launch.
