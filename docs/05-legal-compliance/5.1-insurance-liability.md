## 1. Risk Landscape

Every charging session involves a stranger's vehicle on private property, connected to electrical infrastructure. The four primary risk categories are:

| Risk | Likelihood | Severity | Primary Liable Party |
|------|-----------|----------|----------------------|
| Electrical fault / surge damaging EV battery | Low | High | Host / Platform |
| Slip/trip/fall over charging cable | Medium | Medium | Host / Platform |
| Charger fire / electrical hazard | Very Low | Critical | Host / Platform |
| Vehicle collision on host property | Low | Medium | Driver (auto policy) |
| Property damage by driver (landscaping, gate) | Low | Medium | Driver |
| Charger hardware theft / vandalism | Low | Low–Medium | Platform (optional) |
| Billing dispute / overcharge | Medium | Low | Platform |
| Data breach (personal / payment data) | Low | High | Platform |

## 2. Three-Tier Insurance Model

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  TIER 1: Driver's Own Auto Insurance                │
│  Covers: Vehicle damage, at-fault collisions        │
│  on private property. Primary for moving incidents. │
└──────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                       │ gaps covered by ↓
┌──────────────────────▼──────────────────────────────┐
│  TIER 2: Zipgrid Platform Umbrella Policy           │
│  $1M–$5M Commercial General Liability (CGL)         │
│  Covers: Third-party bodily injury, property        │
│  damage, electrical fault claims during sessions.  │
│  Activates when host or driver policy is exhausted. │
└──────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                       │ underlying property covered by ↓
┌──────────────────────▼──────────────────────────────┐
│  TIER 3: Host's Own Property Policy                 │
│  Homeowners / Renters / Commercial Property.        │
│  Covers: Structural damage to host property.        │
│  (Host must disclose Zipgrid use to their insurer.) │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. Platform Policy Details

### 3.1 Host Protection Guarantee
Modelled on Airbnb AirCover:

- **Coverage amount:** $1,000,000 per occurrence
- **What it covers:**
  - Third-party bodily injury to a driver occurring during a booked session on host property
  - Property damage caused directly by the charging session (electrical surge, cable trip hazard)
  - Legal defence costs for covered claims
- **What it does NOT cover:**
  - Intentional acts by the host
  - Pre-existing property damage
  - Claims arising from host non-disclosure (e.g. faulty wiring known before listing)
  - Vehicle damage covered under driver's auto policy

### 3.2 Hardware Protection (Optional Add-On)
- **Coverage:** Replacement or repair of host's charger unit
- **Covered causes:** Vandalism, cable cutting, electrical damage from driver misuse
- **Limit:** Up to $2,500 per claim
- **Status:** `[TBD — confirm with underwriter whether bundled or optional premium]`

### 3.3 Driver Terms of Service Obligations
Drivers must accept ToS acknowledging:
- They are using private electrical equipment at their own risk
- Their auto insurance is primary for any vehicular collision on host property
- They are liable for property damage they cause beyond normal wear
- Overstay idle fees are pre-authorised at booking

## 5. Host Onboarding Safety Requirements

Before a listing goes live, hosts must:

- [ ] Confirm charger is less than 10 years old
- [ ] Confirm GFCI / RCD protection is installed
- [ ] Confirm wiring was installed by a licensed electrician (or certify it was)
- [ ] Upload photo of charger showing make/model and cable condition
- [ ] Accept Host Protection Guarantee terms
- [ ] Disclose the listing use to their home insurer (platform provides a template disclosure letter)

         │
         ▼

## 7. Data Liability

| Data Type | Risk | Mitigation |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| Payment card data | PCI-DSS breach | Stripe handles — no raw card data on Zipgrid servers |
| Personal identity (KYC docs) | Regulatory breach | Encrypted at rest, Stripe Identity handles storage |
| Location data (GPS) | Privacy breach | GDPR/POPIA compliant retention & deletion policies |
| Access codes | Unauthorised property access | Encrypted in database, transmitted over TLS only, auto-expired after session |
| OCPP session logs | Industrial espionage / data misuse | Retained 24 months, access restricted to ops team |
