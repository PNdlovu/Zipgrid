## 3. Financial Services & Payments

### 3.1 Payment Services Regulations 2017 (UK PSR — implements PSD2)

Zipgrid **does not hold or transmit funds directly** — all payment processing is handled by **Stripe**, which is FCA-authorised as an e-money institution. This significantly reduces Zipgrid's direct regulatory exposure.

| Obligation | Analysis | Requirement |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **Payment institution authorisation** | Stripe is the regulated entity; Zipgrid is a merchant using Stripe's services | Zipgrid itself does NOT require FCA authorisation for payment services |
| **E-money regulation** | Zipgrid holds no customer funds — Stripe holds all funds in escrow | No e-money licence required |
| **Stripe Connect compliance** | Hosts receiving payouts are Stripe Connect accounts — Stripe performs KYC/AML on hosts directly | Zipgrid must ensure its Stripe Connect integration follows Stripe's Connected Account requirements |
| **Consumer payment protections** | Stripe's terms provide chargeback rights to drivers — Zipgrid must cooperate with Stripe's dispute process | Respond to Stripe dispute requests within their stated deadlines (typically 5–7 days) |

> **Key point:** Zipgrid's legal position is that of a **technology platform** facilitating transactions via an FCA-authorised payment processor, not a payment service provider in its own right. This should be confirmed by legal counsel before launch.

### 3.2 Anti-Money Laundering (AML)

| Obligation | Requirement | Status |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| **AML programme** | Basic AML policy covering customer due diligence, suspicious activity reporting | 🔴 Required pre-launch |
| **Customer due diligence (CDD)** | KYC verification via Stripe Identity covers basic CDD for hosts | ✅ Covered by Stripe |
| **Enhanced due diligence (EDD)** | Required for hosts with very high earning volumes (> £10,000/month) — manual review | 🟡 Policy to define |
| **Suspicious activity reporting** | If Zipgrid becomes aware of suspicious transactions: obligation to report to National Crime Agency (NCA) via SAR | 🔴 Policy required pre-launch |
| **Record keeping** | Transaction records retained 5 years | ✅ DB retention policy covers this |

### 3.3 Consumer Rights Act 2015 & Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013

| Obligation | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Right to cancel** (14-day cooling off) | Booking cancellations within 14 days of purchase require refund unless service has already been performed (session completed). Zipgrid's cancellation policy must clearly state this. |
| **Digital service terms** | Subscription terms must clearly state price, renewal terms, and how to cancel |
| **Price transparency** | Total cost (including fees, VAT) shown before payment — no hidden charges |
| **Unfair contract terms** | ToS reviewed to ensure no terms that would be considered unfair under the Act |

## 4. Electrical Safety & Charger Standards

### 4.1 BS EN 61851 — Electric Vehicle Conductive Charging System

The international/UK standard for EV charging equipment safety.

| Part | Description | Zipgrid Obligation |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| BS EN 61851-1 | General requirements for EV charging | Listings must only feature chargers compliant with this standard. Host safety checklist confirms compliance. |
| BS EN 61851-21-1 | EMC requirements for on-board chargers | Not a direct Zipgrid obligation — applies to EV manufacturers |
| BS EN 61851-22 | AC EV charging stations | All Level 2 AC chargers on platform must comply |

**Zipgrid's role:** Platform, not charger manufacturer. Compliance obligation sits primarily with the charger manufacturer. Zipgrid's obligation is to ensure listed chargers are from compliant manufacturers and to remove listings where hardware safety concerns are reported.

### 4.2 PAS 1899:2022 — Electric Vehicle Smart Charging

The UK standard for smart EV chargers (OCPP-based) published by BSI.

| Requirement | Zipgrid Impact |
|-------------|---------------|
| Smart chargers sold after 30 June 2022 must support OCPP | Confirms Zipgrid's OCPP integration strategy is aligned with current UK standard |
| Smart chargers must support demand-side response | Validates Zipgrid's smart scheduling feature as standards-compliant |
| Smart chargers must have randomised delay on startup | OCPP service should not command simultaneous start for large numbers of chargers (grid protection) |
| Data security requirements for networked chargers | OCPP security specification (WSS, authentication) covered in `3.4-ocpp-integration.md` |

### 4.3 OZEV (Office for Zero Emission Vehicles) — Electric Vehicle Homecharge Scheme

| Regulation | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| EVHS grant compliance | Chargers installed under the EVHS grant must meet minimum standards. Hosts who used the grant have compliant hardware by definition — Zipgrid can use this as a quality filter. |
| OZEV-approved charger list | Zipgrid's supported charger brands (EO, Rolec, Andersen, Ohme, Zappi, Wallbox) are all OZEV-approved — no action required. |
| Workplace Charging Scheme (WCS) | Commercial hosts who installed via WCS have compliant hardware. Platform can partner with OZEV to promote Zipgrid to grant recipients. |

### 4.4 IET Wiring Regulations — BS 7671 (18th Edition)

The UK standard governing all electrical installations. Relevant to hosts' charger installations.

| Requirement | Zipgrid Obligation |
|-------------|-------------------|
| EV charging installations must be carried out by a qualified electrician | Host safety checklist requires self-certification of professional installation |
| RCD (Residual Current Device) protection required | Host checklist confirms RCD protection is in place |
| EVSE installation must be notified to local authority (Building Regulations Part P for domestic) | Inform hosts of this obligation in onboarding; provide template letter if needed |

### 4.5 Health & Safety at Work Act 1974 / Occupiers' Liability Act 1957

| Risk | Zipgrid Requirement |
|------|---------------------|
| Slip, trip, fall over charging cable | Host checklist requires cable management. Zipgrid insurance covers this during sessions. |
| Electrical injury during session | Covered by platform CGL insurance. Host checklist minimises risk through hardware standards. |
| Commercial host visitor safety | Commercial hosts have independent H&S obligations under the 1974 Act for their premises — Zipgrid's role is to ensure the charger itself is safe, not the broader premises. |

## 5. Consumer Protection & Trading Standards

### 5.1 Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008

| Prohibition | Zipgrid Obligation |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Misleading actions | Charger specs (kW, plug types, speed) must be accurate. Automated check: OCPP telemetry confirms actual max power vs. listed power. |
| Misleading omissions | All costs (session fee, idle fee, peak surcharge) displayed before booking — no hidden charges. |
| Aggressive commercial practices | No dark patterns in booking flow (pre-ticked add-ons, obscured cancellation, deceptive urgency). UX guidelines prohibit these. |

### 5.2 Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) — Online Platforms Review

Zipgrid operates as a marketplace and may fall within scope of the CMA's digital markets work.

| Consideration | Requirement |
|--------------|-------------|
| Fake reviews | Review integrity policy (see `4.2`) prevents manipulation. No incentivised reviews. |
| Ranking transparency | If sponsored/featured listings are introduced (Phase 2): clearly labelled as "Sponsored". |
| Price comparison | Nearby comparable listings shown honestly — not manipulated to make Zipgrid listings appear cheaper. |
| Terms fairness | ToS reviewed to ensure platform does not impose unfairly one-sided terms on hosts. |

### 5.3 Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) — CAP Code

| Requirement | Zipgrid Application |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Accuracy | Earnings claims in ads (e.g. "Earn up to £300/month") must be achievable and based on real data. Use actual host earnings data with appropriate caveats. |
| Price claims | "Half the price of public charging" type claims must be substantiated with data from identified comparators. |
| Social media | Influencer/affiliate posts about Zipgrid must be disclosed as paid partnerships (#ad). |

## 6. Employment & Contractor Law

### 6.1 Installer Marketplace Classification

The legal classification of installers on the Zipgrid marketplace is critical — misclassification as employees rather than independent contractors carries significant liability.

| Test | Analysis |
|------|---------|
| **Substitution** | Installers can send a substitute (another qualified person) — supports self-employment |
| **Control** | Zipgrid sets safety and quality standards but does not control how jobs are done — supports self-employment |
| **Mutuality of obligation** | No obligation for Zipgrid to offer work or installer to accept — supports self-employment |
| **Integration** | Installers maintain their own businesses and serve other clients — supports self-employment |

**Assessment:** Installers on the marketplace are **independent contractors**, not employees or workers. Zipgrid's platform agreement with installers must clearly establish this and include appropriate indemnity clauses.

> ⚠️ **This assessment must be reviewed by an employment solicitor before marketplace launch.** IR35 and the Supreme Court's Uber judgment mean gig economy classification is under heightened scrutiny.
