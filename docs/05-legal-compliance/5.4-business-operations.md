## 1. Company Formation Checklist

### 1.1 Legal Entity

```
□ Register Zipgrid Ltd with Companies House
  → Form IN01 (online via Companies House portal)
  → Registration fee: £12 (online)
  → Processing time: 24 hours
  → Required: director name, registered address, SIC code
  → SIC code: 62012 (Business and domestic software development)
              or 74909 (Other professional, scientific and technical activities)

□ Choose registered address
  → Options: director's home address OR registered agent service (~£50/year)
  → Recommendation: use registered agent to keep personal address private

□ Memorandum and Articles of Association
  → Use model articles (Table A) initially — customise before Series A

□ Shareholder agreement (founder shares)
  → Document equity split before any investment
  → Vesting schedule: 4 years, 1-year cliff (standard)
  → Engage solicitor for this — do not DIY

□ Trademark registration
  → "Zipgrid" — UK Intellectual Property Office (UKIPO)
  → Classes: 35 (marketplace), 37 (installation services), 42 (software/tech)
  → Fee: £170 for one class + £50 per additional class = £270 total
  → Timeline: 4 months typical
  → Also register: zipgrid.co.uk (domain) + zipgrid.app + zipgrid.io
```

### 1.2 Tax & Financial Registration

```
□ Apply for Corporation Tax registration
  → HMRC online — within 3 months of starting trading
  → Corporation tax: 25% on profits (2026 rate; small profits rate 19% under £50k)

□ VAT registration
  → Mandatory when turnover exceeds £90,000/year
  → Voluntary registration recommended from launch (reclaim VAT on expenses)
  → Register for VAT MOSS (Mini One-Stop Shop) for digital services to EU consumers
  → VAT scheme: Standard (quarterly returns)

□ PAYE registration
  → Required when first employee is hired
  → Real Time Information (RTI) submissions with every payroll run

□ Employer's NI + Pension
  → Auto-enrolment pension scheme required for all employees
  → Employer contributes minimum 3% of qualifying earnings
  → Provider: Nest (free for employers) or salary sacrifice scheme

□ Business bank account
  → Open before any transactions
  → Options: Tide, Monzo Business, Starling Business, Barclays
  → Recommendation: Tide or Starling (fast, API-friendly, good integrations)
  → Required for Stripe Connect business verification
```

## 2. Banking & Financial Operations

### 2.1 Business Bank Account Structure

```
Zipgrid Ltd — Primary Account (Tide/Starling)
  ├── Operating account (day-to-day expenses)
  ├── Tax reserve account (set aside 25% of profit monthly)
  └── Emergency reserve (maintain 6-month runway minimum)
```

### 2.2 Stripe Account Structure

```
Stripe Platform Account (Zipgrid Ltd)
  ├── Collects driver payments
  ├── Deducts platform commission
  ├── Releases host earnings to Connected Accounts
  └── Transfers Zipgrid revenue to business bank account (weekly)

Stripe Connect Accounts (one per host)
  └── Host receives their net earnings here → transfers to personal bank
```

### 2.3 Financial Controls

```
□ Accounting software: Xero (£15–40/month depending on plan)
□ Accountant: ICAEW/ACCA-qualified firm (recommend fintech-experienced)
□ Bookkeeping: monthly reconciliation minimum; weekly during growth phase
□ Management accounts: monthly P&L, cash flow, runway
□ Annual accounts: filed at Companies House within 9 months of year end
□ VAT returns: quarterly (due 1 month + 7 days after period end)
□ Expense policy: company card via Tide/Soldo for team; Xero receipts
□ Payroll: monthly via Xero Payroll or Brightpay
□ Director's salary: consider IR35-optimised structure (salary + dividends)
   → Get accountant advice on this before setting up
```

## 3. Insurance Procurement Checklist

These policies must be in place **before any user accesses the platform**.

### 3.1 Required Policies

| Policy | What it covers | When needed | Est. Annual Cost |
|--------|---------------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Corporate CGL (£5M)** | Claims against Zipgrid itself (hosts and drivers keep their own cover — 5.1 v0.2) | Before paid marketing | £8,000–£25,000 |
| **Directors & Officers (D&O)** | Claims against directors for wrongful acts | Before Series A / investor involvement | £2,000–£5,000 |
| **Professional Indemnity** | Claims arising from advice or services | At launch | £1,500–£4,000 |
| **Cyber Liability** | Data breach, ransomware, GDPR fines | Before handling personal data | £2,000–£6,000 |
| **Employers Liability** | Employee injury claims | When first employee hired (legally required) | £500–£1,500 |

### 3.2 Insurance Broker Brief

When approaching brokers, provide:
- Business description: P2P EV charging marketplace (technology platform, not charger operator)
- Revenue model: transaction commission + subscriptions (not energy supplier)
- User base: residential hosts + commercial hosts + EV drivers
- Geographic scope: UK (expanding to EU Phase 3)
- Transaction volume projections (Year 1–3 from 1.5-revenue-model.md)
- Platform uptime and technical controls (from 3.5-security-privacy.md)
- Host safety requirements (from 4.2-trust-safety-policy.md)

Target brokers with sharing economy / marketplace / P2P experience:
- Howden Group (sharing economy specialists)
- Lockton (P2P marketplace experience)
- Gallagher Specialty (technology + marketplace)
- Markel (specialty tech)

```
├── Head of Partnerships (BD)
└── Customer Success Manager

Month 12–18 additions:
├── Data Engineer
├── Security Engineer
├── Finance Manager
└── Legal Counsel (in-house or on retainer)
```

### 4.2 Employment Framework

```
□ Employment contracts: written contracts for all employees (UK law requirement)
□ Contractor agreements: separate IR35-assessed agreements for contractors
□ Background checks: all engineering and finance staff
□ IP assignment: all employees sign IP assignment clause (code belongs to Zipgrid Ltd)
□ Confidentiality / NDA: signed at offer stage
□ Equity / options: EMI share option scheme (HMRC-approved, tax-efficient for UK employees)
□ Probation period: 3 months (standard)
□ Notice period: 1 month (increasing with seniority)
□ Working from home policy: fully remote-first; optional London office when appropriate
□ Holiday entitlement: 25 days + bank holidays minimum
□ Pension: auto-enrolment from day 1

□ Payroll provider: Xero Payroll or Brightpay
□ HR platform: Rippling or Personio (as team grows)
□ Benefits: private health insurance (Bupa/AXA) from Month 6; life insurance Phase 2
```

### 4.3 Remote Work & Security Policy

All team members must:
- Use company-managed devices or enrol personal devices in MDM (Jamf/Kandji)
- Connect via VPN for any production database access
- Use 1Password Teams for credential management
- Enable full-disk encryption on all devices
- Complete annual security awareness training (via KnowBe4 or Curricula)
