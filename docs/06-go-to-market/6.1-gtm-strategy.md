## 1. GTM Philosophy

Zipgrid does not compete on features incumbents already have. The GTM strategy is built around three positions none of them can claim:

> **"The only EV charging app that thinks, speaks, and earns for you."**

Every marketing message, partnership, and channel choice flows from this positioning. We are not another charger map. We are not another monitoring app. We are the platform that turns a passive asset (your charger) into an active participant in the energy economy — controlled by your voice.

---

## 2. Target Customer Priority

### Supply-side first (hosts before drivers)
The fundamental marketplace problem: drivers won't come if there are no listings; hosts won't list if there are no drivers. Zipgrid resolves this by **seeding supply before opening demand**.

Launch sequence:
```
Month 1–2: Recruit 200 homeowner hosts (London)
Month 2–3: Recruit 50 SMB hosts (London)
Month 3:   Open to drivers (300 listings live before first driver campaign)
Month 4–5: Dual-sided growth (host + driver acquisition simultaneous)
Month 6:   Evaluate and expand to Manchester
```

### ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) by segment

**Homeowner Host ICP:**
- London / suburban area
- Owns EV (any brand) with Level 2 home charger
- Installed charger in last 3 years (OZEV grant likely used)
- On a time-of-use tariff (Octopus Go, Agile, or EOn Drive)
- Works outside home Mon–Fri (charger idle during day)
- Household income > £45,000 (tech-comfortable, motivated by passive income)

**SMB Host ICP:**
- London café, gym, or retail with 3–20 parking spaces
- Already has at least 1 EV charger installed
- Has noticed EV drivers as a growing customer segment
- Owner-operated or manager who controls commercial decisions
- Turnover £200k–£2M (resource-constrained — no dedicated IT team)

**Driver ICP:**
- London resident without driveway (flat/terrace, on-street parking)
- Drives EV daily (commuter or urban driver)
- Currently spending > £80/month on public rapid charging
- Aged 28–50, tech-comfortable (iPhone or mid-range Android)
- Frustrated with public charger reliability and cost

## 4. Acquisition Channels

### Channel 1 — Installer Partnership (Highest ROI, Phase 1)

**Mechanic:** OZEV-certified installers bundle Zipgrid with every new charger installation. When they fit a customer's charger, they walk them through the Zipgrid app setup on the spot. Homeowner is already in the buying mindset; charger is fresh; motivation is highest.

**Incentive:** Installers receive a referral fee (£15–£25 per host activation) + verified installer badge + marketplace listing (inbound leads).

**Target:** 50 installer partnerships in London in Month 1. 200 nationally by Month 6.

**CAC impact:** Near-zero cash CAC for hosts acquired via installer channel. The installer does the work.

---

### Channel 2 — Energy Supplier Co-Marketing

**Mechanic:** Partner with Octopus Energy (and EDF, EOn in Phase 2) to co-market Zipgrid to their EV tariff customers. These customers have already made the EV switch, are already on a smart tariff, and are exactly the homeowner host ICP.

**Offer to energy supplier:** Their customers get a better smart charging experience (Zipgrid integrates natively with Octopus Agile rates). Octopus gets to offer more value with no build cost.

**Distribution format:** Email inclusion in Octopus EV welcome sequence + in-app mention in Octopus app + co-branded landing page.

**Reach:** Octopus has ~600,000 EV tariff customers. A 0.5% conversion = 3,000 potential hosts.

## 5. Pricing Strategy

### Freemium → Conversion Funnel

```
FREE TIER
(Drive trial adoption — low friction)
     │
     ├── Homeowner: monitors charger, sees earnings potential → upgrades for Smart features
     ├── Driver: browses map, sees listings → first booking converts them to paying customer
     └── SMB: connects charger, sees basic data → upgrades for analytics + dynamic pricing
     │
PAID TIER
(Revenue begins)
     │
     ├── Homeowner Smart: £7.99/month (target: 10–15% of free users within 90 days)
     ├── SMB Starter: £25/month (target: 60% of SMBs within 60 days)
     └── P2P commission: 10–15% per session (automatic — no upgrade needed)
```

### UK-Specific Pricing Rationale

| Competitor benchmark | Price | Zipgrid |
|---------------------|-------|---------|
| Pod Point app | Free (hardware lock-in) | Free + paid tier |
| Zap-Map Premium | £2.49/month (mapping only) | £7.99/month (voice + AI + P2P) |
| Checkatrade (installer leads) | £80–£120/month | 12% commission (performance-based) |
| bp pulse rapid charging | £0.79/kWh | Zipgrid P2P: avg £0.36/kWh (host-set) |

Zipgrid is cheaper for drivers, more powerful for hosts, and fairer for installers than every existing option.

## 6. Retention Strategy

### Host Retention
- Weekly payout notification ("£94 is on its way to your bank") — most powerful retention trigger
- Monthly earnings report email with benchmarks ("You earned 23% more than the average London host")
- Superhost programme — status badge drives listing visibility and host pride
- Proactive outreach when listing goes 7 days without a booking → pricing suggestions

### Driver Retention
- Post-session summary: kWh + £ saved vs. public rapid + CO₂ avoided — makes every session feel good
- Favourite listings feature — reduces search friction for repeat bookings
- Recurring booking (same slot weekly) — converts one-time users to habitual users
- Push notification when a saved listing becomes available

### SMB Retention
- Monthly analytics email digest — even if they don't open the app, they see the value
- AI pricing nudges ("Enable surge pricing this Friday — local event expected nearby")
- Peer benchmarking ("Your café's charger utilisation is 41% — top Manchester cafés average 68%")
