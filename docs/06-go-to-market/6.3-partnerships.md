## 1. Partnership Philosophy

Every Zipgrid partnership must satisfy at least two of the following:

1. **Distributes Zipgrid to our ICP** at low or zero marginal CAC
2. **Deepens the product** (data, integration, or feature) that competitors cannot replicate
3. **De-risks the platform** (insurance, compliance, infrastructure)
4. **Creates lock-in** that makes switching away from Zipgrid costly for hosts or partners

Partnerships are not logo collections. Every one has a measurable target and a 90-day activation plan.

## 2. Tier 1 — Critical Partnerships (Must have at launch)

### 2.1 Octopus Energy

**Type:** Energy supplier + smart tariff data partner  
**Priority:** 🔴 Must have at launch  
**Why:** Octopus has ~600,000 UK EV tariff customers. They are the dominant smart EV tariff provider. Native Octopus Agile API integration makes Zipgrid's smart scheduling meaningfully better for the largest cohort of UK homeowner hosts.

**What Zipgrid offers Octopus:**
- A smart charging platform that makes their tariff product stickier
- Ability to tell customers "Zipgrid automatically charges your car at 7p/kWh"
- Co-branded content that positions Octopus as the premium EV energy partner

**What Octopus offers Zipgrid:**
- API access to live Agile half-hourly rates (already publicly available but formalise partnership)
- Email inclusion in their EV tariff welcome sequence (~150k EV customers/year)
- Co-branded landing page on octopus.energy
- Potential for joint press release

**Commercial structure:** Revenue share on Zipgrid hosts who are also Octopus customers and upgrade to Smart tier (£1.50 per new Smart subscriber originated via Octopus channel)

**Activation plan:**
- Week 1: Intro via BD contact or warm intro through UK EV community
- Week 2–4: Technical integration scoping (Agile API already documented)
- Week 4–6: Co-marketing proposal with commercial terms
- Week 6–8: Legal sign-off
- Week 8: Integration live; co-marketing campaign brief starts
- Month 3: First joint email campaign to Octopus EV base

**KPI:** 1,000 Zipgrid Smart subscribers acquired via Octopus channel by Month 12

### 2.2 OZEV-Certified Installer Network

**Type:** Installation channel + marketplace supply  
**Priority:** 🔴 Must have at launch  
**Why:** Installers are the highest-ROI acquisition channel for homeowner hosts. They interact with the exact ICP at the exact moment of maximum motivation (charger just installed, electrically live, standing in the driveway).

**Target:** 50 London installers in Month 1 → 200 nationally by Month 6

**Partner profile:**
- OZEV-certified (registered with OZEV grant scheme)
- Based in launch cities (London priority)
- Installs residential or SMB chargers
- Already using a job management tool (ServiceM8, Tradify, or similar)

**What Zipgrid offers installers:**
- Verified Installer badge on marketplace profile (builds trust vs. Checkatrade etc.)
- Inbound job leads from platform fault detection (agentic referrals)
- £15–£25 referral fee per host they activate on Zipgrid
- Simple white-label onboarding card to hand to new customers at installation

**What installers offer Zipgrid:**
- Host acquisition at near-zero CAC
- Charger brand knowledge (which models are most common in their patch)
- Trust proxy (homeowner trusts the installer who fitted their charger)

**Recruitment channels:**
- OZEV installer register (publicly accessible list)
- NICEIC, NAPIT, and SELECT (Scotland) installer directories
- EV industry events (EV Charging Summit, SMMT EV Forum)
- Direct outreach via LinkedIn

**Activation plan:**
- Month -1: Identify 100 London installers; contact via phone + email
- Month -1: 50 sign partnership agreement; receive onboarding pack + referral cards
- Month 1: Zipgrid marketplace installer profiles live; first referrals tracked
- Month 3: Evaluate performance; expand to Birmingham + Manchester cohort

**KPI:** 150 hosts acquired via installer channel in first 6 months; £0 paid CAC per installer-referred host (only referral fee on activation)

### 2.3 Insurance Underwriter

**Type:** Risk partner  
**Priority:** 🔴 Must have at launch (non-negotiable — platform cannot open to public without this)  
**Why:** The platform umbrella policy (£1M CGL) is a core product promise to hosts. Without it, host acquisition is severely hampered and the platform carries unacceptable liability exposure.

**Requirements for underwriter:**
- Commercial General Liability (CGL) cover: £1M–£5M per occurrence
- Coverage for: bodily injury + property damage during OCPP charging sessions on private property
- P2P / sharing economy platform experience preferred
- UK-authorised insurer (FCA regulated or Lloyd's syndicate)

**Target underwriters:**
| Underwriter | Rationale |
|------------|-----------|
| Markel UK | Specialty tech/marketplace + sharing economy experience |
| Hiscox | SMB/tech specialty; strong UK platform |
| Tokio Marine Kiln | Lloyd's syndicate; energy + technology specialty |
| CNA Hardy | Specialty liability; P2P / gig economy experience |
| Next Insurance UK | Digital-first; small business focus |

**Broker approach:** Engage a specialist MGA or Lloyd's broker with P2P/platform experience first — they shop the risk to multiple underwriters simultaneously. Target brokers: Howden, Lockton, Gallagher specialty.

**KPI:** Policy placed and active before any public listing goes live

## 3. Tier 2 — High-Value Growth Partnerships (Phase 1–2)

### 3.1 EO Charging

**Type:** Hardware OEM + installer network  
**Why:** EO is the most commonly installed home charger in the UK (particularly via OZEV grants). Deep OCPP integration at launch + EO endorsement significantly builds trust with homeowner hosts.

**Approach:** Technical integration partnership — EO lists Zipgrid as a compatible platform in their app and documentation. Zipgrid features EO as "featured compatible charger."

**What each side gets:**
- EO: more value for their hardware customers → reduces churn from EO ecosystem
- Zipgrid: reach into EO's ~200,000 installed base via in-app referral

**Commercial:** Non-exclusive, non-revenue-share in Phase 1. Just technical compatibility and co-marketing.

---

### 3.2 Rolec EV

**Type:** Hardware OEM (commercial/SMB focus)  
**Why:** Rolec is dominant in commercial installations (car parks, workplaces, hospitality). Their customer base maps directly to Zipgrid's SMB ICP.

**Approach:** Similar to EO — OCPP integration + mutual referral. Target Rolec to include Zipgrid in their post-installation SMB customer communications.

---

### 3.3 Andersen EV

**Type:** Premium residential hardware OEM  
**Why:** Andersen targets the premium homeowner segment (£1,500+ chargers). These customers are high-income, tech-forward, and motivated by premium features — exactly the target for Zipgrid Smart tier.

**Approach:** Zipgrid as the recommended "smart management platform" for Andersen customers. Andersen includes Zipgrid in their product packaging and welcome email sequence.

---

### 3.4 myenergi (Zappi)

**Type:** Hardware OEM (solar/renewable focus)  
**Why:** Zappi is popular with eco-conscious homeowners who have solar panels. These users already think about energy intelligence — Zipgrid's smart scheduling and P2P earning is a natural extension.

**Approach:** Integration partnership + Zappi community (myenergi have an active user community). Explore whether P2P earnings can be framed as "your solar-charged car earns for you" — powerful narrative.

---

### 3.5 EDF GoElectric / EOn Drive

**Type:** Energy supplier (non-Octopus EV tariff customers)  
**Why:** Covers EV tariff customers who are NOT on Octopus (~400,000 UK EV drivers on other smart tariffs). Critical for homeowners who want smart scheduling but use EDF or EOn.

**Approach:** Same structure as Octopus — API integration + co-marketing. Secondary priority after Octopus is confirmed.

## 4. Tier 3 — Strategic Long-Term Partnerships (Phase 2–3)

### 4.1 Local Councils — London Boroughs

**Why:** Councils are under pressure to hit EV infrastructure targets. Zipgrid's private charger network helps them meet those targets without capital expenditure. Several London boroughs have active EV strategy teams.

**Offer to councils:**
- Zipgrid listing data as a free layer on council EV maps
- Joint comms to residents about earning from home chargers
- Community EV charging programme co-delivery (Phase 3)

**Target boroughs (London):** Hackney, Southwark, Lambeth, Wandsworth, Islington (all have active EV/sustainability leads)

---

### 4.2 National Grid ESO

**Why:** National Grid's Electricity System Operator is actively building smart charging programmes to manage grid demand. Zipgrid's network of smart-charger data is exactly the kind of distributed resource they need.

**Opportunity:** Demand Response programme — Zipgrid hosts earn a small incentive for delaying charging during peak grid stress events. National Grid pays Zipgrid for participating nodes.

**Status:** Phase 3 (requires scale — minimum ~5,000 active smart chargers to be worth ESO's engagement overhead)

---

### 4.3 Property Developers

**Why:** All new UK residential builds with >10 parking spaces must include EV charge points (Building Regulations Part S, 2022). Property developers need a management platform for those charge points. Zipgrid as the default platform in new-build properties creates instant supply.

**Target:** Barratt, Taylor Wimpey, Persimmon, Berkeley Group, Urban Splash (new-build focused)

**Offer:** Zipgrid SDK/API embedded into new developments. Residents get Zipgrid pre-configured — zero onboarding friction. Developer differentiates with "smart charging included."

---

### 4.4 Fully Charged / EV Media Partnerships

**Why:** Fully Charged Show is the UK's largest EV content platform (3M+ YouTube subscribers). Their audience is exactly Zipgrid's ICP — EV early adopters, homeowners, tech-forward.

**Offer:** Sponsorship + authentic product integration (not just logo placement). Host a "How I earn £150/month from my charger" segment.
